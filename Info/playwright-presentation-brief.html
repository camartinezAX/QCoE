🚨 Why We Need to Move

Critical limitations with TestIM that are impacting our efficiency

🔴 Critical: No Scheduling Queue System

TestIM lacks a queue system. When test executions exceed our parallel limit, it causes multiple test failures. Their only solution is "buy more parallels"—a band-aid that costs more money without solving the root problem.

🔒 Vendor Lock-In

Tests are proprietary. If we leave, our tests are completely unusable—years of work, gone.

💸 Ongoing Costs

~$150K/year in subscription fees. We pay but never own what we create.

🐌 Limited Execution

Only 1 test at a time on our own servers. Must use their Grid for parallel runs.

🤖 No AI Tooling

Cannot leverage Cursor AI or other modern development accelerators.

❓ Common Questions

💡 Is the framework ready?

+
Yes. We built it based on our existing mobile framework. OLB 3.0 will start fresh with Playwright—no migration needed.

🔄 What happens to existing TestIM tests?

+
They continue running. Teams using TestIM can keep using it. Over time, we can negotiate fewer parallel slots or phase it out entirely.

🛡️ Is Playwright enterprise-ready?

+
Absolutely. Built and maintained by Microsoft (same team as VS Code). Industry standard, used by major enterprises worldwide.

🎯 Why not just buy more TestIM parallels?

+
That's a band-aid, not a solution. It doesn't fix vendor lock-in, AI limitations, or test ownership. We'd be paying more to stay locked into a platform that limits our growth.

🤝 Can developers contribute to tests?

+
Yes! Tests are JavaScript—the language developers already know. This creates shared quality ownership between QE and Development teams.
